Publications

Direction-Anchored Decoupled Dirichlet Evidential Learning for Reliable Graph Node Classification
First Author · ICIC 2026 (CCF-C) · Oral Presentation 2026

Accepted by Lecture Notes in Artificial Intelligence (LNAI). We propose a direction-anchored decoupled Dirichlet evidence modeling method, which separates category direction and evidence strength, and integrates temperature scaling, prototype density and graph structure propagation to improve model calibration and uncertainty estimation.

RCD-EMT: Robust Community Detection in Noisy Networks via Evolutionary Multitask Optimization
Third Author · IEEE TETCI (JCR Q1, IF=6.5) Under Review

We propose an evolutionary multitask framework for robust community detection. Through synchronous evolution and bidirectional knowledge transfer between community detection and structure enhancement, it achieves accurate and robust community identification in noisy networks.
